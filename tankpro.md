# TankPro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 20, 2026_

TankPro ("the app") is a game published by BornHunter Games, a brand of Omnia
Stream LLC. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What we collect

- **Anonymous player ID.** When you use the online leaderboards, the app signs
  you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID
  that is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.
- **Leaderboard entries.** For each leaderboard (Endless and Campaign) we store
  one row per player: an auto-generated display name (for example
  "Player4821"), your best score, the highest level you reached, and the time
  the row was last updated. The app never asks you to type a name.
- **Technical data.** Google's Firebase services may process technical
  information such as your IP address as part of delivering the service.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, left-handed layout, turning sensitivity,
screen shake, FPS counter), campaign progress, best score for each level, and
your generated display name are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app
removes them.

## What we do not do

The app has no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and does not collect
your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any personal information you
type. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google
(Firebase), which processes it on our behalf to run the leaderboards.

## Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept until you ask for them to be removed. To request
deletion, email us at the address below with your display name (shown on the
leaderboard) and we will delete the entry. Uninstalling the app does not by
itself remove the leaderboard entry, because it is not linked to any account.
Reinstalling the app creates a new anonymous ID and a new display name.

## Children

The app is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone. Because nothing personal is requested, no age
verification is needed.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
