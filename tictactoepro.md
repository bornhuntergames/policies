# TicTacToePro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

TicTacToePro ("the app") is a game published by BornHunter Games. This policy
explains what the app stores and why.

## What we collect

- **Anonymous player ID.** When you use the leaderboard, the app signs you in
  anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID that is
  not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.
- **Leaderboard entry.** For the leaderboard we store an auto-generated display
  name (for example "Player4821"), the largest grid size you reached, and the
  time it was last updated. The app never asks you to type a name.
- **Technical data.** Google's Firebase services may process technical
  information such as your IP address as part of delivering the service.

## What stays on your device

Your best score and your generated display name are also saved locally on your
device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## What we do not do

The app has no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and does not collect
your location, contacts, photos, or any personal information you type. We do
not sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google (Firebase), which
processes it on our behalf to run the leaderboard.

## Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept until you ask for them to be removed. To request
deletion, email us at the address below with your display name (shown on the
leaderboard) and we will delete the entry. Uninstalling the app does not by
itself remove the leaderboard entry, because it is not linked to any account.

## Children

The app is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone. Because nothing personal is requested, no age
verification is needed.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
